Support

Need a hand with BinDayNI, or spotted something wrong? We'd love to hear from you.

Email us

weeappsni@gmail.com

The app also has built-in buttons (in Settings) to report a wrong bin date, report a bug, or suggest a feature — these pre-fill an email with the details we need to help.

Bin dates look wrong?

Dates come from your council's own bin-lookup service. If a date looks off, email us your council area and postcode and we'll look into it. In the meantime, your council's website is the final say.

BinDayNI is an independent app and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any council.
