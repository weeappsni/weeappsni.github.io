BinDayNI

Free bin collection reminders for Northern Ireland.

What it does

Enter your address and BinDayNI works out your council's collection schedule, shows which bin is next and when, and reminds you the night before. If your council isn't auto-detected, you can set your bins up manually — it never leaves you stuck.
